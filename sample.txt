Chapter 1

Case jacked into the Grid, praying the ICE wouldn't fry his neural pathways before he could grab the data. The dealer's deck blinked red across the bar.

"You're late," the dealer said, not looking up from his deck. Neon spilled across his face from the noodle-stand sign outside.

Case ignored him. He had three minutes before the data slot closed. Three minutes to either walk out rich or be carried out twitching.

Chapter 2

The rain in Chiba never stopped. It was the kind of rain that ate concrete and rusted the cheap chrome on every shop sign within a kilometer. Case pulled his coat tighter.

"Tell me you got it," Molly said over the comm. Her voice was flat, the way it always was when she was angry.

"I got it," he said. The drive was warm in his pocket. Too warm.
